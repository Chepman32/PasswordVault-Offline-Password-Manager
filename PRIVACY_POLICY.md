# Privacy Policy for PasswordVault

**Last Updated: October 26, 2025**

## Introduction

PasswordVault is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data handling practices for the PasswordVault mobile application ("App").

## Our Commitment

**PasswordVault is 100% offline and collects NO data.**

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Period.

### What We DON'T Collect

- ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- ❌ Password data
- ❌ Usage analytics
- ❌ Device information
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Advertising identifiers
- ❌ Crash reports
- ❌ Any other data

## Data Storage

All data created in PasswordVault is stored **exclusively on your device**:

- Passwords are encrypted using AES-256 encryption
- Encryption keys are stored in the device's Secure Enclave (iOS) or Keystore (Android)
- No data is ever transmitted over the internet
- No cloud backup (unless you manually export and backup)
- No synchronization between devices

## Third-Party Services

PasswordVault does NOT use any third-party services, including:

- Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
- Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics)
- Advertising networks
- Social media integrations
- Cloud storage providers
- Any other external services

## Internet Access

PasswordVault does NOT require or use internet connectivity:

- The app works completely offline
- No network requests are made
- No data is uploaded or downloaded
- No remote servers are contacted

## Biometric Data

PasswordVault uses your device's biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) for app access:

- Biometric data (fingerprints, face scans) is NEVER accessed by PasswordVault
- We only receive a success/failure result from the operating system
- All biometric data remains in the device's Secure Enclave
- We cannot access or export your biometric information

## Data Export

You can export your password data using the app's export feature:

- Export is entirely optional and user-initiated
- Exported files are saved locally to your device
- YOU are responsible for the security of exported files
- We recommend encrypting and deleting exported files after use

## Data Security

We take security seriously:

### Encryption
- All passwords are encrypted using AES-256
- Encryption keys are stored in the device's Secure Enclave (iOS) or Android Keystore
- Passwords are never stored in plain text

### Access Control
- Biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) or PIN required
- Auto-lock after inactivity
- Option to lock on background

### Local-Only
- All data stays on your device
- No cloud synchronization
- No backup to external servers

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data:

- There is no data to access
- There is no data to delete
- There is no data to transfer
- There is no data to correct

All data is under YOUR control on YOUR device.

## Children's Privacy

PasswordVault does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The app is suitable for all ages.
